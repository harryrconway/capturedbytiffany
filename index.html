01 Behind the Lens

Based on the Gold Coast and working throughout Australia and internationally, Tiffany is a photographer focused on capturing people, places and spaces with a natural, considered approach.

With a background spanning lifestyle and people, architecture, travel and hospitality, the work is driven by an appreciation for light, detail and the atmosphere of a place. Whether photographing a person, a finished space or a destination, the aim is always the same: to create images that feel authentic, refined and true to the experience.

From intimate portraits and creative sessions to architecture, hotels and travel, Tiffany works closely with clients to create imagery that feels genuine rather than overly produced.

Gold Coast, Australia. Available internationally.
